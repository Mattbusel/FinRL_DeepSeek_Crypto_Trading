Bitcoin trading bot research: three DQN agents and a DeepSeek news signal

Three small AI agents that learned to buy, sell or hold Bitcoin every 2 seconds, plus an honest replay of how they did.

For people learning deep reinforcement learning for trading, FinRL contest entrants, and anyone curious how an LLM sentiment score plugs into a trading agent.

Research code, not financial advice. Nothing here has traded real money. The results come from one training seed on nine days of 2021 data. Crypto trading can lose all of the capital you put in.

Try it in one command

No data download and no API key. About 15 seconds on a laptop CPU. It replays the trained agents over 4 bundled hours of the real contest test data.

How one decision is made

  1. The state. Every 2 seconds the agents get 12 numbers: their position and holding time, 8 factors an RNN extracts from 101 order-book alphas, and the latest DeepSeek sentiment and risk score (1 to 5, neutral 3 when there is no headline).
  2. The agents. D3QN, DoubleDQN and TwinD3QN are 3-layer, 128-wide networks. Each scores sell, hold and buy and votes for its best score.
  3. The vote. The majority wins. The contest simulator caps the position at 1 BTC, stops out on moves against the best price, closes after an hour, charges slippage, and books the change in value as the reward.

What the demo reveals: D3QN wants to trade, the other two learned to always hold, and TwinD3QN turned out to be an exact copy of DoubleDQN, so the 3-agent ensemble never trades.

Reproduce the full run in 3 steps

The market data is public (1.4 GB, hosted by the contest organisers). Training takes about 30 minutes on a CPU.

pip install torch --index-url https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cpu
pip install numpy pandas scipy matplotlib pydantic pydantic-settings pyyaml openai gdown

# 1. download the data and split it 70/30 in time
python scripts/prepare_data.py --download --source data/raw
# 2. train the three agents on the first 70%
LARSA_DATA_DIR=data/train NUM_SIMS=256 RL_BREAK_STEP=60000 python task1_ensemble.py
# 3. replay them over the last 30%, hour by hour
LARSA_DATA_DIR=data/test python scripts/replay_test.py

Configuration, metrics, the DeepSeek news pipeline and every file are described in the reference.
